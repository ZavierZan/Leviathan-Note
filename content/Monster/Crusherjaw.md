
**Type**:: Creature / Entity  
**Classification**:: Fogbound Aberration
**Threat Level**:: Moderate (Boss)

---
## 📜 **Description**

Top-heavy and hulking, Crusherjaw’s silhouette is dominated by a grotesquely oversized **right arm**, knotted with muscle and thick as a tree trunk. It has **no neck**; instead, its face is embedded deep in its chest, a **gaping mouth lined with jagged, boulder-crushing teeth**.

Its left hand is stunted, good only for crude grabs, while a single **oversized, unblinking eye** bulges from the left side of its head — a rarity among Fogbound. Every step it takes makes the ground quake, scattering dust and loose stone.

---

## 🧠 **Lore / Origin**

Once a miner, desperate to save his trapped child from a quarry collapse. He sought strength where no man should, whispering into the fog for help. The Being answered — and the price was more than he could carry.

Now, Crusherjaw is bound to the quarry, forever “digging” for something only it can see.

---

## 🧩 **Behavior Patterns**

- Heavy, thunderous footsteps that shake terrain.
- Will not leave its quarry territory, but **kills anything** that enters it.
- Smashes obstacles with overwhelming force, creating new hazards for the player.
- Throws debris or chunks of stone when at range.

---

## 🔀 **Forms / Stages**

- As it takes damage, **fog seeps deeper into its body**, swelling its size and amplifying its strength.    
- Can only be seen if Charlotte’s sanity is below ~20% **or** if a special artifact/glyph is active.

---

## 🗣️ **Perception**

- **Player**: A living wall of muscle and stone, its movements shaking the screen.    
- **Charlotte**: A nightmare given form, a monster she must defeat to press onward.
- **Townsfolk**: Claim “there’s nothing down in the quarry” — they’ve never seen it.

---

## 📍 **Known Habitats**

- Deep within the rocky quarry mine.    
- Highly territorial — will never leave its home.
 
---

## ⚠️ **Threats / Abilities**

- **Sheer Strength** — crushes rock, wood, or metal alike.    
- **Terrain Destruction** — alters the battlefield mid-fight, creating hazards and bottlenecks.
- **Pursuit** — slow but relentless; once it gives chase, it won’t stop until you leave its range or kill it.

---

## 🛡️ **Weaknesses**

- Repelled by **wards and safe zones**.    
- Vulnerable to **iron tools** — steel slides harmlessly from its hide.
- Best counter: stay out of range; run, don’t hide.

---

## 🎮 **Function (Gameplay Purpose)**

- Serves as a **major boss** and a hard roadblock.    
- Forces the player to learn about **Enchantment Glyphs** to progress.
- Breaks walls and environmental barriers to corner the player.
- Optional clue: understanding the glyph will help to counter it.

---

## 🔍 **Clues / Loot**

- Body is **tattooed with ancient glyphs** granting strength enhancement.    
- Dropped loot: a fragment of its glyph-skin, usable to unlock a glyph deciphering.

---

## 🧩 **Notes**

- Only boss-level Fogbound have visible **eyes** — a sign they still “see” their prey directly.
- Encounters should feel like being **trapped in a collapsing quarry**.

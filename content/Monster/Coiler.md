
**Type**:: Creature / Entity  
**Classification**:: Aquatic Stalker / Ambusher  
**Threat Level**:: High

---

## 📜 Description

Being near it feels like drowning on dry land — the air grows thick, wet, and heavy, making every breath a struggle. Moisture beads on your skin, seeping into clothes, clinging until it feels as though the fog itself is trying to pull you under. The scent of brine and decay lingers in its wake.

---

## 🧠 Lore / Origin

Once a fisherman of Townia, now an abomination bound to the fog’s will. It is obsessed with **catching** something — what that “something” is, no one knows. Some say it will leave you alone if it has already claimed its desired prize, though no one who has been caught has returned to confirm this. Old glyphs, carved into stone near the docks, serve as warnings of its presence — crude depictions of tentacles, drowning figures, and the deep.

---

## 🧩 Behavior Patterns

- Lies motionless in the fog until a target is near, then strikes without warning.
- Relocates and “camps” after each successful catch.
- The air around it becomes unnaturally damp, leaving behind slick, soggy traces.
- Uses tentacles to seize prey, dragging them toward hidden water sources.

---

## 🔀 Forms / Stages

- **Low Fog Density**: Difficult to spot, but faint ripples in puddles or water surfaces may give it away.    
- **High Fog Density**: Nearly invisible; only a shimmer or distorted air hints at its location.
- **Sanity Influence**: The lower the player’s sanity, the more aggressive it becomes. Severing one of its tentacles causes two to regrow, each faster and stronger.

---

## 🗣️ Perception

- **Player**: Can occasionally glimpse its silhouette when it moves in invisibility.
- **Charlotte**: Sees it as a monstrous, amphibious figure with writhing tentacles.
- **Townsfolk**: Attribute disappearances to wild animals or freak accidents at sea.

---

## 📍 Known Habitats

- Any location with water — wells, beaches, the river, rocky tide pools.
- Migratory, following sources of moisture and fog density.

---

## ⚠️ Threats / Abilities

- Can remain perfectly still in thick fog, becoming effectively invisible.
- Produces a sticky, flammable goop that hampers movement.
- Can extend tentacles through the fog to snatch prey from a distance.

---

## 🛡️ Weaknesses

- **Fire**: The goop coating its body dries quickly when heated and ignites easily.    
- **Environmental Avoidance**: Avoiding bodies of water reduces encounter chances.

---

## 🎮 Function (Gameplay Purpose)

- Acts as an **elite predator** in the fog, requiring special tactics to counter.    
- Forces players to manage positioning near water and use environmental hazards.
- Ties into environmental clues and glyph warnings for pre-emptive avoidance.

---

## 🔍 Clues / Loot

- Pools of viscous, unidentifiable slime.    
- Damaged fishing nets, hooks, and scales not belonging to any known sea creature.

---

## 🧩 Notes

Highly territorial, but its “territory” shifts constantly. Survivors speak of hearing water splashing from impossible distances before it strikes.

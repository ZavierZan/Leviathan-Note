
**Type**:: Creature / Entity  
**Classification**:: Fogbound Collector  
**Threat Level**:: low -> extreme  

---

## 📜 **Description**

A tall, sharply dressed gentleman in a **steampunk tailcoat** and **tophat**, walking with a black cane capped in silver. At a glance, he appears human enough — polite posture, fluid movements, even the faint air of nobility. But there’s something wrong: his **shadow lags behind**, his breath never mists in the cold, and his **eyes burn faint red** when he glances your way.

---

## 🧠 **Lore / Origin**

No one remembers when Coldman first appeared in [[Townia]] — only that he always seems to be where he’s most needed… or least expected. Some whisper he’s a **Watcher gone astray**, others think he’s the last merchant of the Fogbound, trading not for gold but for something far more personal.

The truth? Coldman is a **Collector** — a Fogbound entity tasked with gathering memories, knowledge, and fragments of sanity for the Being. His deals feel fair, but each exchange tilts the balance between human and monster… and always in his favor.

---

## 🧩 **Behavior Patterns**

- Moves like an ordinary man, always calm and deliberate.
- Fights with grace, using melee or ranged attacks based on player position.
- The air around him grows **unnaturally cold** when he’s near.

---

## 🔀 **Forms / Stages**

1. **Merchant Form** – Offers goods, quests, or cryptic warnings. Friendly demeanor.
2. **Shifted Form** – When Charlotte’s sanity drops or after repeated deals, becomes distant and unnerving. Speech turns sharper.
3. **Final Form** – Reveals full Fogbound nature; cane becomes a conduit for freezing fog and shadow-portals.

---

## 🗣️ **Perception**

- **Player**: Feels subtly wrong — shadow out of sync, movement too smooth.    
- **Charlotte**: Possibly a Watcher… but his eyes are wrong.
- **Townsfolk**: “Oh, him? Just a traveling gentleman.” "Coldman? Who? Ahh, you mean that gentleman? He didn't talk much but, he's a honest trader"

---

## 📍 **Known Habitats**

- Wanders Townia after a key event.    
- Appears in different spots when player is stuck or low on resources.

---

## ⚠️ **Threats / Abilities**

- **Ice Manipulation** – Freezes terrain, slows movement.    
- **Memory Alteration** – Erases or distorts small details in the world.
- **Shadow-Whip Cane** – Extends to strike at range, snapping like a serpent.
- **Portal Hat** – Opens small rifts; uses them to attack from unexpected angles or grab the player.
- **Area Freeze** – Slams cane down to instantly frost the ground.

---

## 🛡️ **Weaknesses**

- In early game, not aggressive unless provoked.    
- Vulnerable to **heat-based attacks** and weapons made out of iron in combat.
    
- Becomes less predictable the more deals the player makes.
    

---

## 🎮 **Function (Gameplay Purpose)**

- **Early Game** – Friendly NPC, selling supernatural items and providing cryptic hints about anomalies.
    
- **Mid Game** – Starts testing the player, questioning their motives.
    
- **Late Game** – Becomes a major boss; difficulty scales with how much you’ve relied on his aid.
    
- The more you **die**, the more wary and hostile he becomes.
    

---

## 🔍 **Clues / Loot**

- Early: Special consumables, rare lore fragments, quest items.
    
- Boss: Fragment of his cane (weapon upgrade) and a **Fog Shard** for major story progression.
    

---

## 🧩 **Notes**

- Serves as the player’s **most trusted “friend”** before becoming one of their most dangerous foes.
    
- Personality is polite and well-spoken, but turns predatory when sanity is low.
    
- A master of **calculated patience** — lets the player dig their own grave before striking.

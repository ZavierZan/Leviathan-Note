
**Type**:: Creature / Entity  
**Classification**::   Stalker
**Threat Level**:: Low  

---

## 📜 Description
Tall, impossibly slender figures with elongated limbs ending in sharp, splinter-like claws. Their movement is accompanied by an unnatural _creaking_ sound — a rhythmic grind like warped wood under strain, produced by grinding their jagged teeth together. They are almost always heard before they are seen, and even then, only at the very edges of perception.

---

## 🧠 Lore / Origin
Creekers are remnants of something once human, though their exact origin is unknown. Their forms are unstable, constantly shifting as if reality cannot hold them in place. They cannot be perceived by those fully sane — their presence is a fracture in the mind’s ability to process reality. Only those touched by the fog’s influence, or slipping toward madness, can begin to hear their creaking call.

---

## 🧩 Behavior Patterns
- **Primary Role**: Stalker and indirect guide.
- Tracks its target from a distance, often without direct confrontation.
- Will follow for hours or days before making its move.
- Uses sound to mislead or lure prey into certain areas.

---

## 🔀 Forms / Stages
- **Low Sanity**: Visible in distorted flashes, moves erratically.
- **Mid Sanity**: Only audible, creaking growing louder the closer it is.
- **Full Sanity**: Completely invisible, sound faint and intermittent — may be mistaken for the environment.

---

## 🗣️ Perception
- **Player**: May catch brief visual flickers and directional audio cues.
- **Charlotte**: Hears strange creaking noises but rationalizes them as wind, old wood, or distant construction.
- **Townsfolk**: Rarely notice — if they do, they dismiss it entirely (“old trees shifting in the wind”).

---

## 📍 Known Habitats
- Prefers wooded areas and places with natural ambient noise to mask its approach.
- Migratory, wandering freely around Townia but lingering near glyph sites and deep in forest.

---

## ⚠️ Threats / Abilities
- **Auditory Manipulation**: Distorts and misdirects sound to lure prey or cause confusion.
- **Stalker Persistence**: Will trail a target indefinitely until conditions are right to strike.
- **Psychic Pressure**: Prolonged exposure increases paranoia and sanity drain.

---

## 🛡️ Weaknesses
- Avoidance: If the creaking grows louder, moving in the opposite direction greatly reduces encounter risk.
- Glyph wards tuned to sound distortion may repel it temporarily.

---

## 🎮 Function (Gameplay Purpose)
- At **full sanity**, acts as a subtle guide, luring the player toward hidden clues or lore objects.
- As sanity **drops**, becomes increasingly hostile, actively hunting and distorting audio cues.
- Serves as both a breadcrumb mechanic and an escalating threat — following its sound may lead to rare rewards, but at significant risk.

---

## 🔍 Clues / Loot
- Deep claw marks carved into tree bark, sometimes forming partial glyphs.
- Unnatural creaking sounds that persist even when wind is absent.

---

## 🧩 Notes
Its role shifts depending on the player’s mental state — ally, omen, or executioner.

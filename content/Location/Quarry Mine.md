**Region**: Western rocky terrain  
**Access**: Open, but deeper sections restricted

---

## 📜 **Description**

The Quarry Mine is still in operation — on the surface. Workers chip away at stone and unearth copper and iron deposits. But deeper within, the tunnels run quiet. The mine’s collapse from months ago is officially labeled a “structural accident,” but many in town suspect otherwise. Old Man Jeofrey, the foreman, keeps work going… and questions to a minimum.

---

## 🧠 **Lore / History**

- Ten months ago, a **shaft breach** revealed something older than stone — a tunnel carved with glyphs.
    
- The collapse was **engineered by Jeofrey**, who then sealed the breach and resumed normal operations.
    
- Miners report **hearing whispers** through the rock.
    
- Jeofrey keeps the damaged helmets locked in a shed. One of them is wet on the inside.
    

---

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Known NPCs**

- [[Old Man Jeofrey]]  — Quarry Foreman. Gruff and unshakable. Claims “nothing’s down there.”
    
- **Luis (Missing Miner)** — Supposedly left town. His locker is still locked. Keys are in Jeofrey’s pocket.
    

---

## 🔦 **Points of Interest**

- **Surface Dig Site**: Active work zone. 
    
- **Old Shaft Entrance**: Locked and boarded. Marked “UNSTABLE” in red chalk.
    
- **Tool Shed**: Holds evidence Jeofrey denies exists.
    
- **Echo Tunnel**: Workers say the rock “calls back in another voice.” Entry is restricted — but not secure.
    

---

## ☠️ **Known Dangers**

- Areas near the sealed shaft distort **sound and direction**.
    
- Workers have gone home with **scratched arms and no memory** of how they got hurt.
    
- Reports of **breathing rock** are dismissed as “airflow pressure.”
    

---

## 🔍 **Clues or Items Found**

- **Broken Helmet with interior glyph scratchings**
    
- **Echo Hammer**: When struck, reverberates phrases in Deep Glyph
    
- **Jeofrey’s Dossier**: Half-burned paper mentioning “replacements in the lower crew”

## 🧩 **Function (Gameplay Purpose)**

- Lore environment where reality begins to shift
    
- Charlotte may use equipment to **record glyph resonance**

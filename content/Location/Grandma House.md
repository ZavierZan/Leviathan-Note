# Grandma House

**Region**: Northern edge of town, forested residential outskirts  
**Access**: Unlocked after arrival

---

## 📜 Description

An abandoned home at the northern tip of Townia. Though the previous owner passed years ago, the house seems maintained — swept floors, warm air, and the smell of old wood. Charlotte lived here in her childhood. Now it’s a haven — but only for a time. The fog doesn’t quite reach inside… yet.

---

## 🧠 Lore / History

- Charlotte’s grandmother once lived here and was part of a **warding ritual**.
    
- Certain rooms seem _unaged_; a sign that time or memory might be anchored inside.
    
- “This house doesn’t remember me. It remembers _something.”
    

---

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Known NPCs

- None (but filled with memories. More than normal)
    

---

## 🔦 Points of Interest

- **Attic Music Box**: Plays a tune from Charlotte’s dreams — not that she remembers.
    
- **Photo Wall**: One face blurred out in every frame.
    
- **Basement Door**: Locked tight. Maybe she can find a way somehow.
    
- **Garden Patch**: Soil smells metallic. Iron rods buried deep.
    

---

## ☠️ Known Dangers

- The basement is tight shut. She can't tell what's in it
    
- Sleep here too often and dreams begin replacing reality.
    
- A mirror doesn’t reflect fog — but _something else._
    

---

## 🔍 Clues or Items Found

- **Burned Letter**: Grandma’s final warning.
    
- **Music Box Key**: Found beneath floorboard.
    
- **Partial Ritual Diagram**: Reveals placement of protective glyphs.
    
- **Old Sketch of Townia** — includes structures that don’t exist anymore.
    

---

## 🧩 Function (Gameplay Purpose)

- **Player home base / rest area**
    
- Unlocks core **memories**, **flashbacks**, and **ritual history**
    
- Only the safe place from the fog — don't know how long it will last

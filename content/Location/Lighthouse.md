# Lighthouse

**Type**:: Location  
**Region**:: Coastal cliff, the tallest place in the town  
**Access**:: Restricted  

---

## 📜 Description

Standing tall on the western cliff, the lighthouse is Townia’s highest structure — once a beacon for wayward fishing boats, now a pulsing anomaly. Its light no longer rotates normally; instead, it pulses in deliberate intervals, sometimes flickering to the rhythm of something unseen offshore. The ocean crashes violently below, and no one comes up here unless they must. The path to the lighthouse is steep and exposed, often swallowed by fog. Despite its height, the fog here seems denser than anywhere else in town.

---

## 🧠 Lore / History

- Randal has not left the tower in **over 4 months**.
    
- He claims the light “no longer guides — it warns.”
    
- Some nights, the light spins counterclockwise. Some nights… it doesn’t spin at all.
    
- Logs describe **phantom ships** that follow the beam but vanish before reaching shore.
    
- The structure predates the official founding of Townia. Some suspect it was **built atop something else.**

## 🔦 Points of Interest 

- **Light Chamber**: At the top of the tower; features heavy gears, unstable mechanisms, and glyph carvings beneath rusted panels.
    
- **Randal’s Living Quarters**: Scattered with torn ship logs, half-functioning radios, and maps covered in red wax markings.
    
- **Observation Platform**: A narrow balcony with a fogged lens viewer. You may glimpse something crawling along the waves.
    
- **Service Hatch**: Rusted shut, sealed with a broken keypad. Unlockable later — leads to a maintenance crawlspace or hidden lower floor.
---

## ☠️ Known Dangers

- Possible **dream exposure** while sleeping inside.
    
- Reports of **time stutter** near the observation lens — memories loop or stutter.

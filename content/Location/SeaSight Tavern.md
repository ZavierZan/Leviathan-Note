**Region**: Lower town, near coastal path  
**Access**: Always open

---

## 📜 Description

A cozy inn nestled near the track to the ocean. Warm lights, old wood, and the ever-present smell of fish stew. The last place that pretends everything is normal. But there are whispers in the kitchen, and the mirror in the dining hall never fogs — no matter how thick the mist outside.

---

## 🧠 Lore / History

- Built on the foundation of a **lifeboat house**.
    
- The tavern mirror was salvaged from a wrecked ship. Some believe it sees _too much_.
    
- The owner’s daughter is the only person still seen cleaning **Grandma’s house.**
    

---

## 🔦 Points of Interest

- **Dining Hall Mirror**: Always shows one more person than is present.
    
- **Bulletin Board**: Gossip and rumors — some shift each day.
    
- **Private Room**: Rentable room that alters from time to time.
    
- **Pantry Door**: Locked. Unruly sounds behind it.
    

---

## ☠️ Known Dangers

- **Replaced NPCs** may gather here mid-game.
    
- The mirror sometimes “shows” about the user that they can't remember.
    
- Eating the wrong dish may trigger hallucinations.
    

---

## 🔍 Clues or Items Found

- **Cryptic Menu Card**: Lists dishes with glyph-based names.
    
- **Scratched Wooden Plank**: Weirdly attracting
    

---

## 🧩 Function (Gameplay Purpose)

- **NPC hub**, early quest start point
    
- Option to **borrow flashlight** or gather town rumors

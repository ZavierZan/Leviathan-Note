## 📜 Description

Standing tall on the western cliff, the lighthouse is Townia’s highest structure — once a beacon for wayward fishing boats, now a pulsing anomaly. Its light no longer rotates normally; instead, it pulses in deliberate intervals, sometimes flickering to the rhythm of something unseen offshore. The ocean crashes violently below, and no one comes up here unless they must. The path to the lighthouse is steep and exposed, often swallowed by fog. Despite its height, the fog here seems denser than anywhere else in town.

---

## 🧠 Lore / History

Randal has not left the tower in months. He speaks of “eyes in the fog” and “boats without hulls.”  The light no longer attracts ships — instead, it repels them. Or something near them.  Some nights, the light fails to cast shadows.

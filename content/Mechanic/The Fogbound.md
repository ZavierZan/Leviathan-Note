### **Origins**

The Forgotten were not always monsters.  
Once, they were **humans** — settlers, scholars, and wanderers — who came into contact with **the Being**.  
They never knew its form, never saw its face, never heard its voice as we might understand it.  
Instead, it touched their dreams, guiding them with _visions of impossible symbols_ — the **Deep Glyphs**.

It promised **prosperity**:
- Knowledge beyond human reach.
- Power to bend wind and tide.
- Protection from the storms that claimed so many lives.

The Being gave them more than survival — it gave them _dominion_.

---

### **The Price**

Every gift came with a hidden cost.  
The glyphs themselves were a binding — an oath written into the soul.  
They became **loyal** to the Being, not out of devotion, but by the sheer power of the glyphs rewriting the very patterns of their thoughts.

Over time:

- Their bodies darkened as if the fog seeped into their blood.
    
- Their forms began to warp — tall and thin like shadows, or heavy and hulking like half-formed nightmares.
    
- They struggled to **maintain a human shape**, especially in the deep fog.
    
- Their eyes vanished into black pits, their mouths filled with too many teeth — always grinning, always hungry.
    

---

### **Powers**

The Forgotten move unnaturally, as though the fog carries them.  
They are not bound by normal human limits.

- They can **vanish into the mist** and reappear without sound.
	
- They became one with fog. Their thought are shared by the fog, becoming hive mind like.
	
- They see things no human can — even in the deepest dark.
    
- Their touch carries a **mind-haze**: those who survive an encounter often forget it happened, or suffer the memory “blinks” common in Townia.

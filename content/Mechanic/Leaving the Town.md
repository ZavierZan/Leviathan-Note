### Why does Charlotte or other leave the town

At first, there’s **no physical barrier**.  
No wall. No guards. No warning.  
The road out is still there.  
The bus still runs — at least once in a while.  
But no one truly leaves.

They just… stop trying.

---
### The “Leaving Effect”

People who make plans to leave **feel fine that day**.  
They pack, say goodbyes, even head toward the exit.

But by the next morning, they’re still in town.  
Their luggage is unpacked.  
Their schedule’s forgotten.  
They’re convinced they “changed their mind” — but **can’t recall why**.

	"I was going to visit my cousin in the city, but... I guess not. Things to do. You know how it is."

This isn’t mind control.  
It’s not force.  
It’s **resignation**.  
The fog doesn’t drag people back — it _unroots their will_.
A person cannot decide to leave. The fog will determine when one need to go.

---
### People Who “Left”

Some townsfolk swear **others did leave**.

- "Old Mira? Took the ferry years ago."
- "Mayor's brother? Moved inland. I think."

But no one gets letters.  
No one receives calls.  
Their names fade from conversation like fog in the wind.

And yet… their houses remain untouched.  
Dust-free.  
Food still fresh.  
As if they might walk back in any second.
Or as if someone is living there.

Some of them do come back. But they feel not the same

---
### What Happens If You Try Anyway?

Some **NPCs will try** — players may even help.

- The next day, they’ll be back.  
    Tired. Confused. Apologetic.  
    Their journey will feel like a **dream** they can’t piece together.  
    Some may even **lose time** — waking up a week later with no idea where they were.
    
- Some **Forgotten**
	After they left, no one can remember them. they will straight up deny their existent. It's like they never exist. Their home, belonging, marking they make and memories, disappear from the town.

---
### Gameplay Hook

Charlotte might be partially resistant.

- But every attempt causes **a price**:  
    Time shifts.  
    Sanity spikes.  
    A day _repeats_… slightly altered.  
    She finds herself waking in the same clothes, but her **reflection blinks out of sync.**
    Or waking up with a memory that she can't remember.

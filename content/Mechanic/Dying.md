For most in Townia, **death is a form of escape**.  
The ultimate freedom.  
The release from the fog’s slow suffocation.

Nobody can prevent it.  
Everybody will face it.  
And, in the end, everyone will give into it.

___

### But Not Charlotte

The moment she stepped into the fog, 
It was drawn to her — perhaps because of her dreams, perhaps of simple curiosity, or perhaps for reasons no human could name.

She has **dwelled on things she should never have touched**.  
Followed thoughts she should have left alone.  
Looked too long in the fog that were not meant to be understood.

Now she is bound to the fog.  
When she dies, it will not free her.  
It will pull her deeper.

She is becoming…  
a **Forgotten**.

---

### What Death Means for Charlotte

For her, death is not release.  
It is **displacement**.  
She wakes in the wrong day — sometimes in the past, sometimes in the future, sometimes just as usual, sometime in the wrong place.
The town twists around her.  
NPCs change. Locations shift. The rules fray.

Every time she returns, the fog holds her **a little tighter**.  
Every time, it is harder to tell what is real.  
And every time, the line between Charlotte and the Forgotten grows thinner.

___
### Gameplay

Death is not the end. Never the game over. It is a necessity component. For some outcome, it is a requirement.

When [[Charlotte]] die. She wake up, maybe yesterday, maybe today, maybe tomorrow, or maybe sometime. It is always unpredictable. The town is not safe either, something, someone, someplace or some memory may be changed, replaced, distorted, added, or erased.

Dying cause a heavy toll on [[Charlotte]]'s mind. It can cause her immense pain and trauma that it would cost her sanity. She may retain the memory, or have blurry visions, even might corrupted by false memories.
